About the Institute

Expanding access to research — institutionally, intentionally, and with care.

01 · Who We Are

A nonprofit built around one unfinished problem.

ConnectEd Research Institute is a nonprofit focused on expanding access to research mentorship and academic opportunity for talented, dedicated students who have been left on the outside of existing academic networks.

The Institute develops programs that let students engage with real research environments, build academic skills, and connect with faculty and research communities — the kind of connections that shape a career long after a single fellowship ends.

Our Vision

Empowering emerging scholars by lowering institutional barriers and fostering deep, sustained academic connections.

02 · Beliefs

What we believe.

  • Early access to research experiences can shape an academic trajectory for a decade.
  • Mentorship plays a critical role — far more than a line on a CV.
  • Exposure to real research environments builds confidence and capability.
  • Institutions and faculty can and should play an active role in supporting emerging scholars.
03 · Integrity

Commitment to academic integrity.

ConnectEd maintains internal policies to support professional standards and ethical academic engagement:

  • Responsible program administration.
  • Appropriate use of research support.
  • Alignment with institutional policies and expectations.
