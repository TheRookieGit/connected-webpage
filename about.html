About ConnectEd Research Institute

01 · Who We Are

Who We Are

ConnectEd Research Institute is a nonprofit organization focused on expanding access to research, mentorship, and academic opportunity.

The Institute operates at the intersection of education, research, and equity. Its work is centered on building structured pathways that enable emerging scholars to engage with academic research environments, develop research competencies, and connect with faculty and scholarly networks.

Our Vision

Empowering emerging scholars by lowering institutional barriers and fostering deep, sustained academic connections.

02 · Why We Exist

Why We Exist

Access to research is not equally distributed. Students from under-resourced schools, first-generation backgrounds, and international contexts routinely encounter the same structural barriers — regardless of ability or ambition.

ConnectEd was built to close these gaps — creating structured pathways where none existed before.

Faculty Mentorship

Direct guidance from experienced researchers remains out of reach for students outside well-resourced institutions.

Research Training

Formal exposure to research methods, norms, and expectations is rarely available before graduate school.

Academic Networks

Professional connections that open doors to opportunities are largely distributed through existing institutional privilege.

Early Scholarly Exposure

Engaging with active research during formative years shapes academic identity — yet access is far from universal.

03 · What We Believe

What We Believe

  1. 01

    Access to research experiences should not be limited by geography, institutional affiliation, or socioeconomic background.

  2. 02

    Early exposure to research and mentorship can shape not only academic outcomes, but also confidence, intellectual identity, and long-term opportunity.

  3. 03

    Institutions, faculty, and nonprofit organizations have a shared responsibility to broaden participation in research.

04 · How We Work

How We Work

ConnectEd delivers impact through four interconnected programs, each addressing a distinct barrier to academic access.

  1. 01

    Research Fellowships

    Structured, mentored research placements connecting early scholars with faculty-led research environments.

  2. 02

    Mentorship Programs

    Programs that connect students with researchers and mentors to support academic development and research exploration.

  3. 03

    Research Training & Courses

    Workshops and structured training in research methods, academic writing, and data analysis.

  4. 04

    Scholarly Exchange & Networks

    Initiatives that support collaboration and knowledge exchange across academic communities.

Together, these programs form an integrated ecosystem designed to support scholars at every stage of their academic journey.

05 · Integrity

Commitment to Integrity

As a nonprofit organization, ConnectEd maintains internal policies to support responsible program administration, appropriate use of funds, and alignment with its mission.

Program structures are designed to respect institutional policies and faculty discretion.
