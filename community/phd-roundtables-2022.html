Doctoral Roundtables | 2022

Past event · January 22 & 23, 2022 · Online roundtables

In January 2022, ConnectEd hosted two roundtables in which doctoral scholars from leading universities spoke candidly about life as a PhD student. Doctoral scholars from leading universities on research, doctoral life, and the job search.

Speakers shared what they had learned from research, how they approached the job search, and what they wished they had known before starting a doctorate. Each session drew between 100 and 200 attendees.

What speakers discussed

Speakers weighed the realities of doctoral study honestly, covering both what makes it hard and what makes it worthwhile.

The challenges

  • The opportunity cost of a PhD, and how it fits one’s career goals
  • The academic job market
  • Moving to a new place and building a support network
  • Finding an advisor
  • Preliminary exams

The rewards

  • Being paid to learn and do research
  • Academic and intellectual freedom
  • Research seminars
  • Teaching and professional development
  • For business scholars, career prospects in China and abroad
