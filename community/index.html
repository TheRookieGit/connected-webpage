Community & Academic Pathways

Academic knowledge becomes more useful when it is shared

ConnectEd’s community initiatives bring learners into conversation with researchers, faculty members, and doctoral students. Through workshops, panels, and sustained mentoring, we make space for questions about research, doctoral study, and academic life.

Our Community Initiatives

    Workshops & Webinars

    Focused learning and discussion that help participants understand academic pathways and prepare for their next steps. Our past programming has included webinars related to doctoral applications and academic development.

A Complement to Research Training

Community learning offers something different from a formal course: access to experience, space to ask questions, and a clearer understanding of possible academic paths.

These initiatives complement ConnectEd’s structured research programs without requiring everyone to follow the same route.
