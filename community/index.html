Community & Professional Development

Academic knowledge becomes more useful when it is shared

ConnectEd’s community initiatives are built around professional development. Through workshops, panels, and sustained mentoring, students and early-career researchers learn directly from faculty members and doctoral scholars about research, doctoral study, and academic careers.

Our Community Initiatives

  1. 01

    Workshops & Webinars

    Focused learning and discussion that help participants understand academic pathways and prepare for their next steps. Our past programming has included webinars related to doctoral applications and academic development.

  2. 02

    Academic Pathways Panels

    Professors and researchers share the experiences and choices that shaped their academic journeys. Past conversations have included faculty affiliated with University of California campuses and other U.S. universities.

  3. 03

    PhD Incubator

    A multi-month nonprofit mentoring initiative for prospective doctoral applicants, combining webinars, faculty and doctoral-student mentorship, one-to-one application feedback, and mentor interviews.

Featured past events.

Since 2020, ConnectEd has brought students together with faculty members and doctoral scholars through panels, workshops, roundtables, and sustained mentoring. Below is a selection of that work; each featured event has its own page with the program and speakers.

  1. Mentoring initiative

    PhD Incubator

    More than 20 faculty and doctoral-student mentors supported prospective PhD applicants through an eight-part webinar series, small-group workshops, and mentor interviews.

    • 24 mentors
    • 8 webinars
  2. Panel series

    Professional Development Panel Series

    Twelve professors from U.S. universities shared their paths through doctoral study and into faculty careers, across four field-specific sessions.

    • 12 faculty speakers
    • 300+ live attendees
  3. Workshop

    Writing to Prospective Advisors

    Professor Belle Wang of Sam Houston State University showed applicants how to write clear, effective inquiry emails to prospective advisors.

    • 500+ participants
    • 287 at peak
  4. Roundtables

    PhD Roundtables

    Doctoral scholars from UC Irvine, Columbia, Princeton, and Stanford spoke candidly about research, doctoral life, and the job search in business and the humanities.

    • 2 sessions
    • 100–200 attendees each
