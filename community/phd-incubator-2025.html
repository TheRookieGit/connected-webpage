PhD Incubator | 2025

A sustained mentoring initiative for the next stage of academic development.

In 2025, ConnectEd organized a nonprofit PhD Incubator to support people preparing to apply for doctoral study.

Over several months, faculty members and doctoral-student mentors worked with prospective applicants through webinars, individualized guidance, application-writing feedback, and mentor interviews.

At a Glance

  • Year2025
  • StatusCompleted initiative
  • FocusDoctoral preparation and academic mentorship
  • ParticipantsProspective PhD applicants
  • MentorsFaculty members and doctoral students
  • DurationSeveral months

What the initiative included.

  • Doctoral-preparation webinars addressing topics relevant to PhD applications.
  • Mentoring by professors and doctoral students, bringing different academic perspectives to participants’ questions.
  • One-to-one feedback on application statements and related materials.
  • Individual guidance and support over several months rather than a single session.
  • Mentor interviews sharing experiences and perspectives on academic pathways.

Why a Sustained Format Matters to Our Approach

The incubator was designed to give participants time to ask questions, reflect on their goals, and develop their application materials with feedback.

It complemented research training by focusing on the transition toward doctoral study and participation in academic communities.

Looking ahead.

The 2025 initiative is presented here as part of our past community work. Future editions, eligibility, and participation arrangements will be announced separately when confirmed.
