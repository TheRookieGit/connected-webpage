For Educators & Mentors

Share your experience. Support emerging scholars.

ConnectEd welcomes interest from researchers, educators, faculty members, doctoral students, and subject-matter specialists who would like to contribute to research education or academic-pathway support.

Possible
Roles

Possible roles include methods instruction, mentored projects, faculty-path conversations, workshops, or mentoring within a specific initiative. Participation is matched to program needs, qualifications, availability, and appropriate professional or institutional permissions.

We Respect Contributors’ Privacy

  • There is no requirement to appear in a public faculty directory
  • The role, time commitment, acknowledgment, and any compensation or volunteer arrangement are discussed for the particular activity

Research Rights and Data

  • Research rights, data access, authorship, and confidentiality follow the relevant project agreements and institutional policies
  • We do not presume that a mentor personally owns all research data or student work
