Networks

Scholarly exchange & networks.

Convenings and peer networks that support knowledge exchange across academic communities and emerging scholars.

01 · The Community

A small, working community — not a directory.

Through convenings and peer networks, supporting knowledge exchange across academic communities and emerging scholars — the connective tissue that keeps early-career research moving forward.

Alumni stay connected through a small, working community rather than a credential directory — with recurring reading groups, writing critiques, and advisor office hours.

02 · Activities

What’s on the calendar.

  • Research roundtables — closed-door discussion of works in progress.
  • Writing critique circles — structured peer feedback on drafts.
  • Advisor office hours — drop-in consultation with faculty mentors.
  • Annual convening — short presentations from current fellows.
  • Reading groups — small, topical seminars across disciplines.
03 · Participate

Join a network.

Most network activities are open to current fellows, mentees, and alumni. Outside researchers are welcomed at annual convenings and selected open sessions.
