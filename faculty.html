Advise the Next Generation

For Faculty Advisors

ConnectEd Research Institute invites faculty members and research groups to participate as advisors in the ConnectEd Research Fellowship.

01 · Role

Role of Faculty Advisors

Faculty advisors host selected fellows in structured research experiences aligned with their lab’s work.

Advisors maintain full discretion over:

  • project scope
  • level of engagement
  • expectations and evaluation
  • ownership of research data and publications produced during the fellowship
02 · Program Design

Program Design

The Fellowship is designed to:

  • provide students with access to research environments
  • support faculty in engaging emerging scholars
  • facilitate structured, short-term research participation
03 · Fellows

What Fellows Do

Fellows may:

  • contribute to ongoing research projects
  • support graduate-led work
  • assist with literature review, coding, modeling, or analysis
04 · Institutional Alignment

Institutional Alignment

ConnectEd works to ensure that fellowship placements align with institutional policies and expectations.

Research participation structures are developed in coordination with faculty advisors and, where applicable, institutional guidelines.

05 · Program Support

Program Support

ConnectEd provides:

  • applicant screening and matching
  • program coordination
  • administrative support
  • structured fellowship framework
06 · Research Support

Research Support

Where appropriate and consistent with institutional policy, fellowship placements may include support for research activities within the host lab.
