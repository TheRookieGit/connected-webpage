Advise the Next Generation

For Faculty Advisors

ConnectEd Research Institute invites faculty members and research groups to participate as advisors in the ConnectEd Research Fellowships.

01 — Role

Role of Faculty
Advisors

Faculty advisors host selected fellows in structured research experiences aligned with their lab's work. Advisors maintain full discretion over:

01

Project Scope

The specific research tasks, depth of involvement, and timeline assigned to the fellow.

02

Level of Engagement

The pace, structure, and time commitment expected from the fellow throughout the program.

03

Expectations & Evaluation

Standards, deliverables, and methods of evaluation applied during the fellowships experience.

04

Research Ownership

Full ownership of all research data and publications produced during the fellowships period remains with the advisor.

What Faculty Gain

Why advisors
choose to participate.

Motivated Research Support

Access pre-screened, application-selected fellows ready to contribute meaningfully to ongoing lab work.

Zero Administrative Burden

ConnectEd handles screening, matching, coordination, and program oversight — advisors focus entirely on research.

Expanded Lab Capacity

Supplement your research group with engaged contributors during intensive periods without long-term hiring commitments.

Broader Academic Impact

Shape the next generation of researchers by mentoring talented junior scholars from underrepresented and first-generation backgrounds.

03 — Program Design

Designed for
everyone involved.

The Fellowships are built to benefit students, faculty, and the broader research ecosystem equally.

01

Student Access

Provide students with real access to research environments they would otherwise struggle to reach.

02

Faculty Engagement

Support faculty in meaningfully engaging emerging scholars without adding administrative burden.

03

Structured Participation

Facilitate short-term, structured research participation within a clear and coordinated framework.

Built around
your institution.

ConnectEd works to ensure that fellowships placements align with institutional policies and expectations. Research participation structures are developed in coordination with faculty advisors and, where applicable, institutional guidelines.

Where appropriate and consistent with institutional policy, fellowships placements may include support for research activities within the host lab.
