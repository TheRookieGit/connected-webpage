Accessibility

ConnectEd aims to make its website usable for people with different access needs. Our website is designed to support keyboard navigation, readable text, clear labels, and alternatives for meaningful visual content.

To report an access barrier or request information in another format, contact us and identify the page or feature involved. You do not need to disclose a medical diagnosis. We welcome specific information that will help us understand the issue.
