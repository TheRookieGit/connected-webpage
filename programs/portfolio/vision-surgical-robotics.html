Vision-Guided 3D Reconstruction for Intelligent Surgical Robotics

Investigate how vision, geometric reconstruction, and robot motion relate in an educational robotics study.

Overview

A mentored project explores a bounded problem in spatial perception for robotic systems. Possible work connects geometric reconstruction with kinematic reasoning and technical evaluation. The educational scope is limited to simulation, permitted datasets, or an approved nonclinical setup; it does not involve patient care or surgical experimentation.

Learning goals and possible work

Proposed learning outcomes for this program example:

  • Connect a perception question with a measurable research objective.
  • Develop and evaluate a bounded reconstruction or simulation workflow.
  • Communicate technical limits and nonclinical applicability.

Illustrative project milestones

The sequence below illustrates how this program’s content can be organized. Topics, pacing, and project depth are adapted for each offering. This is not an archived record of a specific cohort’s weekly syllabus.

  1. 01Research question, literature, and nonclinical scope
  2. 02Robot kinematics and spatial coordinate systems
  3. 03Imaging geometry and data preparation
  4. 043D reconstruction workflow and baseline model
  5. 05Linking reconstruction to a bounded perception task
  6. 06Evaluation metrics, simulation tests, and sources of error
  7. 07Robustness checks and technical interpretation
  8. 08Research report and final presentation
