Scalable Manufacturing and Performance Optimization of Flexible Solar Devices

Study manufacturing and performance trade-offs for flexible solar devices through a bounded research project.

Overview

Learners examine how production choices relate to device performance, stability, and scaling. Depending on available resources, the project may use literature-derived evidence, approved process data, or simulation. Any laboratory component requires a separately confirmed facility, safety process, and supervisory arrangement.

Learning goals and possible work

Proposed learning outcomes for this program example:

  • Identify a research question connecting manufacturing and performance.
  • Analyze appropriate evidence or data with stated assumptions.
  • Evaluate scaling or stability trade-offs in a technical report.

Illustrative project milestones

The sequence below illustrates how this program’s content can be organized. Topics, pacing, and project depth are adapted for each offering. This is not an archived record of a specific cohort’s weekly syllabus.

  1. 01Flexible solar devices and the manufacturing question
  2. 02Literature review and performance measures
  3. 03Scalable fabrication concepts and process variables
  4. 04Selecting a data, modeling, or approved experimental approach
  5. 05Analyzing performance and stability
  6. 06Exploring manufacturing and optimization trade-offs
  7. 07Checking evidence and study limitations
  8. 08Research report and technical presentation
