Research Foundations Bootcamp

Build a practical starting point for scholarly reading, literature search, research questions, academic writing, and responsible AI use.

Overview

This introductory program develops the habits and tools needed to begin a research project. Learners practice locating and evaluating scholarship, reading academic articles, synthesizing ideas, and turning an area of interest into a feasible research question. The emphasis is on research reasoning and a defensible plan rather than a publication promise.

Learning goals and possible work

Proposed learning outcomes for this program example:

  • Create a documented search strategy and an annotated reading record.
  • Distinguish a summary from a synthesis and draft a feasible research question.
  • Produce a short research concept note with citations and a responsible-tool-use statement.

Program details

Program type
Research Foundations
Fields
Cross-disciplinary
Methods
Literature search; Critical reading; Synthesis; Question development; Citation; Responsible AI use
Preparation
No previous research experience required. Reading and writing support should be adapted to academic stage.
Format
Online instruction and guided project work; individual or small-group configuration.
Timing
Introductory format: four live classes over approximately 1–2 weeks, plus about 10 hours of practice.

Illustrative learning sequence

The sequence below illustrates how this program’s content can be organized. Topics, pacing, and project depth are adapted for each offering. This is not an archived record of a specific cohort’s weekly syllabus.

  1. 01Finding and evaluating scholarly sources; keeping a search log
  2. 02Reading critically; building a literature matrix and synthesizing evidence
  3. 03Developing research questions; connecting questions, methods, and feasibility
  4. 04Academic writing, citation, and responsible AI use; concept-note presentation
