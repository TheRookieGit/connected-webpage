Research Methods & Data Analysis

Build the tools to answer a research question

Methods programs help learners understand how evidence is produced, analyzed, and interpreted. A program may introduce a new method, develop competence with a research tool, or support an applied analysis within a learner’s field.

Four areas of methods learning.

  1. 01

    Evidence Synthesis

    Systematic reviews and meta-analysis connect careful literature searching and study evaluation with structured evidence synthesis. Learners examine selection decisions, effect sizes, analysis choices, and interpretation.

  2. 02

    Quantitative Inquiry

    Programs may focus on research design, public-data analysis, regression, measurement, or statistical reasoning. Applications can span education, psychology, health, business, and other empirical fields.

  3. 03

    Computational Research

    R, Python, data preparation, visualization, machine learning, and text or network analysis provide tools for a range of research questions. Tool selection follows the question and learner preparation rather than a requirement to use every advanced technique.

  4. 04

    Research Communication

    Focused modules can help learners communicate a project through research writing, a poster, an abstract, or an academic presentation.

How Learning Works

Instruction combines conceptual explanation with practical exercises, guided analysis, independent work, and feedback.

Some offerings culminate in a pilot analysis or research report; others prepare learners for a subsequent project.
