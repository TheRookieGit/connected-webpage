What We Offer

Programs built around real research — and real relationships.

01 · Flagship Initiative

ConnectEd Research Fellowships.

Mentored research, inside the room.

The ConnectEd Research Fellowships support selected fellows in participating in mentored research experiences under the guidance of faculty advisors at leading institutions — with the same expectations and rigor as any graduate-level research placement.

03 · Mentorship

Long-form mentorship pairings.

Sustained mentorship pairings that connect students with researchers for ongoing academic guidance, career navigation, and research exploration — measured in semesters, not sessions.

04 · Training

Research Training & Courses.

Workshops and structured courses in research methods, academic writing, data analysis, and responsible research conduct.

05 · Networks

Scholarly Exchange & Networks.

Convenings and peer networks that support knowledge exchange across academic communities and emerging scholars — the connective tissue that keeps early-career research moving.
