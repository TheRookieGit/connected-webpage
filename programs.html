What We Offer

Programs & Initiatives

ConnectEd Research Institute develops research- and mentorship-oriented initiatives that are offered on a pilot basis and as capacity and funding allow. Our work is designed to strengthen research skills, scholarly networks, and access to academic opportunity.

01 Professional Development
01 Professional Development
01 · Professional Development

Professional Development Dialogues

Virtual webinars and panels connecting students and scholars with researchers and practitioners. Topics include graduate preparation, research design, academic writing, and career pathways.

Pilot events announced periodically; recordings may be shared when available.

02 Mentorship
02 Mentorship
02 · Mentorship

Mentorship & Research Guidance

We pair students with senior mentors — graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and professors — to support academic pathway navigation, research interest development, and higher education applications.

Mentorship opportunities are offered as volunteer capacity allows.

Workshops & Research Training

Workshop models focused on research methodology, academic writing, and data analysis — delivered virtually and occasionally in collaboration with university faculty.
