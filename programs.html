What We Offer

Programs & Initiatives

ConnectEd Research Institute develops research- and mentorship-oriented initiatives that are offered on a pilot basis and as capacity and funding allow. Our work is designed to strengthen research skills, scholarly networks, and access to academic opportunity.

01 · Professional Development

Professional Development Dialogues

The Institute has convened virtual webinars and professional development panels that connect students and scholars with researchers and practitioners. Topics include graduate preparation, research design and methods, academic writing, and academic career pathways.

What to expect: Pilot events announced periodically; recordings may be shared when available.

02 · Mentorship

Mentorship & Research Guidance

We design mentorship approaches that pair students with senior mentors (e.g., graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and professors) to support academic pathway navigation, research interest development, and higher education applications.

What to expect: Mentorship opportunities are offered as volunteer capacity allows.

03 · Workshops

Workshops & Research Training

We develop workshop models focused on research methodology, academic writing, and data analysis. Workshops may be delivered virtually and occasionally in collaboration with university faculty.

What to expect: Workshop offerings vary by funding and partner availability.

04 · Networks

Scholarly Exchange & Networks

We support scholarly exchange by building networks for students, educators, and scholars to share research ideas, collaborate on projects, and build professional relationships across institutions and disciplines.

What to expect: Community-building efforts evolve over time; partnership proposals are welcome.
