01 · Collaborate

Collaboration Opportunities

We welcome collaboration with faculty, scholars, research labs, and academic centers aligned with our mission of expanding access to research opportunities and educational equity. Collaboration models are flexible and can be discussed based on mutual goals, capacity, and timelines.

Possible collaboration models:

  • Co-hosting a virtual research talk, methods workshop, or professional development panel
  • Join us as a faculty advisor for Fellowship recipients — learn more
  • Join us as a faculty mentor for junior scholars
  • Partnering on a small pilot initiative or a sponsored research project

02 · Volunteer

Volunteer Roles

We are actively building volunteer capacity to expand mentoring and program offerings. Volunteers typically contribute in flexible, project-based ways.

Volunteer roles:

  • Fellowship application support (eligibility screening, rubric-based review support)
  • Mentorship coordination (matching logistics, scheduling support)
  • Event support (moderation, outreach, post-event resource posting)
  • Research resource development (toolkits, templates, guides)
  • Communications (newsletter drafts, web updates, social posts)
  • Partnerships outreach (academic centers, labs, faculty networks)

Most roles are flexible and can range from 1–3 hours per month to short-term project sprints during active cycles.

Time Commitment

1–3 hours per month, or short-term project sprints. We work around your schedule.
