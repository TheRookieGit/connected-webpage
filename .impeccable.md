# ConnectEd Research Institute — Design Context

## Users
- **Primary:** Motivated students — especially first-generation, international, and underrepresented — looking for research mentorship and a path into graduate study.
- **Secondary:** Faculty, partner institutions, and donors evaluating whether this nonprofit is serious and worth supporting.
- **Context:** Students browse on phones during the day; donors and faculty read on desktops. The site is a credibility artifact as much as a marketing page.

## Brand Personality
Three words: **rigorous, warm, inclusive.**

Emotional goal: the interface should feel like a real institution (you can trust us with your future) while also signaling that the mission — expanding access — is genuine. Aspirational without being cold.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Editorial-institutional.** Treat the site like a research journal or a well-crafted academic magazine, not a SaaS landing page. References: Harvard Kennedy School, the Aspen Institute, RAND's publication design, long-form pieces in The Atlantic, Princeton's public communications. Light theme — students reading on phones and donors at work both benefit from light, readable surfaces.

Color palette stays: navy (#002b49), deep blue (#005587), gold (#ffb81c), tinted neutrals. Tint all neutrals toward navy so the brand color does quiet work throughout the UI.

**Anti-references:** generic SaaS templates, glassmorphism, rainbow gradients, startup neon-on-dark, crypto/fintech aesthetics, AI-slop cyan glow.

## Design Principles
1. **Editorial, not marketing.** Asymmetric layouts, generous whitespace, serif display type, left-aligned, section numbering.
2. **Quiet authority.** Let typography and composition do the work; avoid "look at me" decoration. Subtle motion, not flashy animation.
3. **Mission in the margins.** Credibility markers (numbers, programs, outcomes) belong in the surface of the UI, not hidden in subpages.
4. **Accessible by default.** Contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support are first-class — this is an equity mission; the UI should live it.
