Expanding Access to Research and Academic Opportunity

ConnectEd Research Institute connects talented students with meaningful research experiences and mentorship within academic environments.

Our Mission

Access to academic resources and research opportunities remains limited for many students. Many capable students—particularly those from underrepresented, first-generation, and international backgrounds—face structural barriers to accessing research environments, faculty mentorship, and academic networks.

ConnectEd Research Institute works to expand access to research environments, mentorship, and academic pathways for students who are motivated to pursue graduate studies, academic careers and contribute to research-driven fields.

Impact Area

Empowering the next generation of scholars through evidence-based mentorship and institutional integration.
