About

Who We Are

ConnectEd Research Institute is a nonprofit organization focused on expanding access to research mentorship and academic opportunity to talented and dedicated students who have limited access to academic resources and research opportunities.

The Institute develops programs that enable students to engage with research environments, build academic skills, and connect with faculty and research communities.

Our Vision

Empowering emerging scholars by lowering institutional barriers and fostering deep academic connections.

What We Believe

  • Early access to research experiences can shape academic trajectories
  • Mentorship plays a critical role in academic development
  • Exposure to real research environments builds confidence and capability
  • Institutions and faculty can play an important role in supporting emerging scholars

Commitment to Academic Integrity

ConnectEd maintains internal policies to support professional standards and ethical academic engagement:

  • Responsible program administration
  • Appropriate use of research support
  • Alignment with institutional policies
