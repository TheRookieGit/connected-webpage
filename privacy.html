Privacy Notice

ConnectEd Research Institute uses information submitted through this website to respond to inquiries, communicate about requested programs or initiatives, and manage updates that people choose to receive.

Information collected

The inquiry form requests a name, email address, inquiry topic, and message, with optional organizational or academic-stage information. Please do not send sensitive student records, health information, confidential research data, or application files through the general inquiry form.

Use and access

Inquiry information is accessed by the people and service providers needed to respond and operate the relevant communication process. It should not be posted publicly. Information is retained only for as long as needed for the stated purpose and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Updates

Subscription to optional updates is separate from sending an inquiry. You may ask to stop receiving updates through the contact process or the unsubscribe option in those messages.

External services

Linked registration, payment, video, or other external services have their own privacy practices. Review the applicable notice before using those services. The website identifies an external provider when that feature is enabled.

Questions

Contact ConnectEd through the published organizational contact channel to ask about your information or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.
