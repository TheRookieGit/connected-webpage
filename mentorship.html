01 · The Pairing

A working relationship, not a meet-and-greet.

Semester-long pairings

Sustained mentorship pairings that connect students with researchers for ongoing academic guidance, career navigation, and research exploration — measured in semesters, not sessions.

Advisors commit to structured check-ins, written feedback on drafts, and concrete deliverable cycles — the conditions under which real scholarship actually develops.

02 · What’s Included

What a pairing looks like.

  • Regular one-on-one meetings across an academic term.
  • Written feedback on research drafts, methods, and analytical choices.
  • Career navigation guidance on graduate school, publications, and research paths.
  • Introductions into broader academic communities, where appropriate.
  • Clear expectations set on both sides — pairings end on a defined review, not a fade-out.
