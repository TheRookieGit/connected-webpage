01 · Mission

Our Mission

ConnectEd Research Institute creates opportunities for talented students who aspire to advance society through research. Our fellowship helps capable students—particularly first-generation students, women, and other underrepresented groups—connect with faculty-led research environments, academic mentors, and scholarly networks where they can build powerful academic and professional careers.

The Institute was established to connect these students with research mentors, faculty-led projects, and scholarly communities where they can build lasting academic foundations. Through fellowship programs, mentorship models, and institutional partnerships, the Institute works to expand access to meaningful academic experiences that support long-term intellectual and professional development.

Our Impact Area

Empowering the next generation of scholars through evidence-based mentorship and deep institutional integration — not shortcuts.

02 · What We Do

What We Do

We design and administer research and mentorship programs that:

  • expand access to faculty-led research environments
  • support structured, mentored academic experiences
  • strengthen research skills and scholarly preparation
  • build connections between students, researchers, and institutions
  • provide financial support that makes participation possible for fellows from all backgrounds
03 · Fellowship

Research Fellowship Program

The ConnectEd Research Fellowship supports selected fellows in participating in mentored research experiences under the guidance of faculty advisors at leading research institutions.

The program is designed to reduce barriers to research access and to support the development of future scholars across disciplines.

Institutional Approach
