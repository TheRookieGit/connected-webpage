01 · Mission

Our Mission

ConnectEd Research Institute creates opportunities for talented junior scholars who aspire to advance society through research. Our programs help capable junior scholars—particularly first-generation junior scholars, women, and other underrepresented groups—connect with faculty-led research environments, academic mentors, and scholarly networks where they can build powerful academic and professional careers.

The Institute was established to connect these junior scholars with research mentors, faculty-led projects, and scholarly communities where they can build lasting academic foundations. Through programs, mentorship models, and institutional partnerships, the Institute works to expand access to meaningful academic experiences that support long-term intellectual and professional development.

Our Impact Area

Empowering the next generation of scholars through evidence-based mentorship and deep institutional integration — not shortcuts.

03 · Fellowships

Research Fellowships Program

The ConnectEd Research Fellowships support selected fellows in participating in mentored research experiences under the guidance of faculty advisors at leading research institutions.

The program is designed to reduce barriers to research access and to support the development of future scholars across disciplines.
