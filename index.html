01 · Mission

Opening the door to research — for the students who weren’t handed a key.

Access to academic resources and research opportunities remains uneven. Many capable students — particularly first-generation, international, and otherwise underrepresented — face structural barriers to research environments, faculty mentorship, and the academic networks that shape a career.

ConnectEd works to expand that access: connecting motivated students with the environments, mentors, and pathways they need to pursue graduate study, academic careers, and research-driven fields.

Our Impact Area

Empowering the next generation of scholars through evidence-based mentorship and institutional integration — not shortcuts.

04 · Approach

How we design programs.

  • Application-based and selective — admitted on merit and fit, not network.
  • Structured with defined expectations, deliverables, and outcomes.
  • Aligned with academic research practices, not a credential-mill shortcut.
  • Designed to support responsible engagement in real research environments.
