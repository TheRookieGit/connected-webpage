ConnectEd Research Institute

Research Education. Mentorship. Academic Possibility.

ConnectEd Research Institute expands access to research learning and academic opportunity. We bring together foundational training, research methods, mentored inquiry, and community-based academic support for learners across secondary, undergraduate, graduate, and early-career stages.

Research learning, at the right stage

A first research question and an advanced project require different kinds of support. Our programs are organized around the nature of the learning experience, with content and expectations adapted to participants’ preparation, interests, and goals.

A Commitment to Thoughtful Research

We emphasize clear research questions, appropriate methods, honest interpretation, and work that learners can explain as their own.

Academic opportunity also grows through community

Our work extends beyond formal courses. Through workshops, faculty conversations, and mentoring initiatives, we help learners understand academic pathways and connect with the experience of researchers at different career stages.

2025 PhD Incubator. A five-month nonprofit initiative in which more than 20 faculty and doctoral-student mentors supported prospective PhD applicants through an eight-part webinar series, small-group application feedback, and mentor interviews.

Workshops & Academic Pathways Panels. Conversations with professors and researchers about their academic journeys and the choices that shaped them.
