ConnectEd Research Fellowship

The ConnectEd Research Fellowship is a structured, application-based program that supports selected fellows in gaining mentored research experience under the guidance of faculty advisors at research institutions.

The Fellowship is designed to expand access to research environments and to support the academic development of emerging scholars.

Mentored research environments
01 · Program Overview

Program Overview

Fellows are selected through a competitive application process and matched with faculty advisors based on academic background, research interests, and program fit.

Research experiences may be conducted remotely or, where appropriate, in person, subject to institutional policies and availability.

02 · Structure

Fellowship Structure

Typical engagements include:

  • 8–12 week research period (or part-time equivalent)
  • structured participation in a research group
  • project-based contributions aligned with ongoing work

Fellows may work directly with faculty advisors or with designated members of the research group, including graduate students or postdoctoral researchers.

03 · Program Support

Program Support

The Fellowship is supported through philanthropic contributions and institutional partnerships that enable fellows to participate in research experiences and support program administration.

04 · Responsibilities

Responsibilities

Fellows are expected to:

  • engage consistently with assigned research activities
  • communicate regularly with their advisor or research group
  • adhere to academic and research integrity standards
05 · Outcomes

Outcomes

The Fellowship provides structured research experience and academic exposure.

Specific outcomes—such as recommendation letters, authorship, or continued collaboration—are determined by the faculty advisor and are not guaranteed.

06 · Eligibility

Eligibility

Applicants should demonstrate:

  • academic readiness
  • interest in a specific research area
  • ability to engage in a structured research environment
