01 · The Program

Mentored research, inside the room.

Real working environments

The ConnectEd Research Fellowships support selected fellows in engaging deeply with mentored research practice under the guidance of faculty advisors at leading institutions.

Placements are structured around defined deliverables, cohort check-ins, and written feedback cycles — not shadowing visits or observational internships.

Explore Tracks Our Approach
02 · What Sets It Apart

The fellowship is structured — not improvised.

  • Application-based and selective — admitted on merit and fit, not network.
  • Paired one-on-one with a dedicated faculty advisor throughout the placement.
  • Structured deliverables: literature review, methods, analysis, written report.
  • Cohort-based check-ins so fellows progress alongside peers, not in isolation.
  • Designed to prepare fellows for graduate study, not to substitute for it.
03 · Who It’s For

Two tracks, one set of standards.

Fellowships are offered across two tracks, matched to a fellow’s academic stage and research interests. Both tracks share the same commitment to mentorship, structure, and rigor.
