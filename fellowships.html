Flagship Initiative

ConnectEd Research Fellowships

The ConnectEd Research Fellowships are a structured, application-based program that supports selected fellows in gaining mentored research experience under the guidance of faculty advisors at research institutions.

The Fellowships are designed to expand access to research environments and to support the academic development of emerging scholars.

At a Glance

  • Selection Competitive, application-based
  • Format Remote or in-person
  • Mentorship Faculty advisor matched
  • Focus Project-based research contributions
Mentored research environments
01 · Program Overview

Program Overview

Fellows are selected through a competitive application process and matched with faculty advisors based on academic background, research interests, and program fit.

Research experiences may be conducted remotely or, where appropriate, in person, subject to institutional policies and availability.

02 · Structure

Fellowships Structure

A structured engagement designed to integrate fellows into active research environments.

01

Program Setting

Summer: 8–12 weeks of intensive engagement.
Semester: 4–5 months of part-time structured participation, providing sustained exposure to a research environment.

02

Group Integration

Structured participation in an active, faculty-led research group with defined roles and responsibilities.

03

Project Contributions

Project-based work directly aligned with ongoing research, supporting real outputs.

Fellows may work directly with faculty advisors or with designated members of the research group, including graduate students or postdoctoral researchers.

03 · Program Support

Program Support

The Fellowships are supported through philanthropic contributions and institutional partnerships that enable fellows to participate in research experiences and support program administration.

Philanthropic and institutional support
04 · Responsibilities

Responsibilities

Fellows are expected to:

  • engage consistently with assigned research activities
  • communicate regularly with their advisor or research group
  • adhere to academic and research integrity standards
Consistent engagement and accountability
Pathways shaped by mentored experience
05 · Outcomes

Outcomes

The Fellowships provide structured research experience and academic exposure.

Specific outcomes—such as recommendation letters, authorship, or continued collaboration—are determined by the faculty advisor and are not guaranteed.

06 · Eligibility

Eligibility

Applicants should demonstrate:

  1. 01

    Academic Readiness

    A foundation of academic preparation relevant to the intended research area, demonstrating capacity to engage with graduate-level concepts and expectations.

  2. 02

    Research Focus

    Clearly articulated interest in a specific research domain, with the ability to connect that interest to ongoing faculty-led work at a research institution.

  3. 03

    Structured Engagement

    Demonstrated ability to operate within a structured professional or academic environment, meeting defined responsibilities with consistency and accountability.
