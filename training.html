Training

Research training & courses.

Workshops and structured courses in research methods, academic writing, data analysis, and responsible research conduct.

01 · Curriculum

Skills fellows actually use.

Methods & writing workshops

Training is designed to complement real placements, not replace them. Fellows leave able to run a literature review, structure a methods section, and defend an analytical choice.

Courses are short-form and workshop-based — built around producing a concrete artifact (outline, memo, analysis plan) rather than passive lecture hours.

02 · Modules

Topics we cover.

  • Literature review — structuring a defensible survey of prior work.
  • Research methods — choosing approaches that match the question.
  • Academic writing — arguments, structure, and clarity for research audiences.
  • Data analysis fundamentals — descriptive statistics, visualization, reproducibility.
  • Responsible research conduct — authorship, attribution, and institutional norms.
